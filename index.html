HVAC Controls Training Station

A wall-mounted training station built from the parts a tech services every day: a CGM09090 field controller, a CVM03050 VAV controller and an XPM04060 on the SA bus, with a network sensor, wall sensor, Belimo LMB24-SR-T actuator, 24 VAC fan, DPM2 panel meters, Zelio relays and Harmony operator devices. Everything mounts on a swing-out panel in a Hoffman CONCEPT 30 × 24 × 12 enclosure. Parts are drawn at true size.

Open items

  1. Power feed: cord and plug (default) or hardwired by an electrician from a building circuit; switch between them in the 3D viewer. A factory-corded supply (PSH100AB10-EXT2) is an alternative that avoids line-voltage terminations.
  2. Relays: CR-1 (fan) and CR-2 (alarm) pre-wired; CR-3 and CR-4 left open for the relay lab.
  3. H-O-A contacts: confirm two ZBE-101 add-on blocks fit the XB5AD33.
  4. Edge grommet: confirm the swing-panel steel gauge suits the grommet range.
  5. nVent: confirm the depth kit (CAM124 for 12 in deep), the 4.00 in setback range and the hinge-axis location.
  6. 120 V feed: cord and plug through a cord grip, primary landed by the electrician; conduit or hard-wire also possible.
  7. TR100VA001: confirm footprint and foot-hole orientation on the backpan.
  8. DPM2 meters: confirm 0-10 V scaling and that the input is isolated from the XPM output common.
  9. Mounting: wall-mount on strut by default; floor stand (AFK2412) available as a customer option. Two horizontal struts; the box bolts through its back mounting holes from inside. Back hole pattern 28.50 x 22.50 in, backpan studs and offset confirmed on the nVent CAD for CSD302412W; struts set 28.50 in apart; mount the box before installing the backpan.
  10. FC bus trunk: confirm trunk baud, unused MS/TP addresses (panel uses 2), trunk device count and cable route. FC BUS A and B (+, −, COM, shield) on blue/white terminals land the site trunk in and out; EOL off unless the panel ends a trunk; shield carried through, not grounded in the panel.
  11. Finder 19.50: confirm US stock.
  12. JCI quote: CGM09090, CVM03050, XPM04060.
  13. Bench-measure: the CVM hub and flow ports.
